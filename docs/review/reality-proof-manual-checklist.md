# Reality Proof — Volkan Visual Gate

## Start

```bash
npm run dev
```

Open `http://localhost:3000/?proof=realities`. The proof is development-only;
production and development without this query resolve to SIGNAL.

## Desktop

- Confirm SIGNAL, GRAVITY and ATELIER read as three independently composed websites.
- Type into the proof input, open the analysis panel, switch EN/DE and then switch realities. Input, panel and selected language must remain intact.
- Switch to GRAVITY without hovering first. The first visit must show either the real field with `COMPUTATIONAL FIELD ACTIVE` or an honest `STATIC FALLBACK`, never an empty canvas or false active label.
- Leave GRAVITY and return. The warm field should resume without a second cold initialization.
- Confirm the field remains clearly visible behind the DOM composition.
- Switch realities slowly. The target must be revealed through the moving Cut while the source remains visible; it must not read as a cover/swap curtain.
- Press `1`, `2`, `3` rapidly in changing orders. The last request must win without a stale URL or intermediate commit.
- Open `LET LENOX DECIDE`, confirm heading focus, choose an answer, close with Escape and confirm focus returns to the trigger.
- Confirm ATELIER is composed around the labelled historical artifact and does not present it as client work.

## Mobile 390×844

- Repeat all three realities and confirm each has a deliberate mobile composition.
- Confirm the Cut runs horizontally without clipping or document overflow.
- Confirm input, controls, analysis panel and LENOX region remain reachable and readable.

## Fallback and motion

- Disable WebGPU and reload the proof. GRAVITY must remain complete, navigable and labelled `STATIC FALLBACK`.
- Enable reduced motion, or open `http://localhost:3000/?proof=realities&lenox-reduced=1`. The transition must settle cleanly without continuous field motion.

## Decision

- `GO`: the architecture may be extended only after the visual criteria above pass.
- `NO-GO`: record the visible failure and keep Phase 1 open. Build and test success alone do not override this gate.
