**Scope:** This contract binds the visible result. Implementation technique is the executor's domain within the Codex-verified boundaries.

## Approved direction

- **Signature Creation: THE COLLAPSE.** GRAVITY is not built — it condenses. After First Contact, LENOX stills, and the void's live matter falls, orbits into a tilted accretion disk, and precipitates into his home. LENOX never gestures; the world moves because he decided.
- **Universal World Switch: THE BLINK.** LENOX closes his eyes; the world's light dies with them; the atomic swap hides in full darkness; the target world's own light performs the reveal. Slice 1 only preserves the seam — the switch itself is a later harness.

## The five beats (desktop ≈ 9–11 s, one master scalar)

1. **Decision (1.5 s)** — First Contact dissolves; LENOX stills; visor drops to one slow instrument pulse; free particles freeze. Silence.
2. **Fall (2.5 s)** — all free matter falls toward a locus behind/below LENOX; streaks; the camera is caught by the same pull.
3. **Accretion (2.5 s)** — matter orbits, flattens into a tilted disk; warm light condenses at the core; the disk plane is secretly the future page plane.
4. **Precipitation (2.5 s)** — particle mass announces typographic lines and architectural plates; real DOM typography resolves the readable contours on the same scalar.
5. **Arrival (1.5 s)** — camera locks to scroll rest pose; LENOX stands *inside* the environment; settled matter keeps drifting slightly downward — the drift is the scroll affordance; visor warmth returns.

**Mobile (≈ 6–7 s):** the same beats recomposed vertically — fall axis straight down the screen, disk edge-on as a glowing horizon band, one readable column, LENOX anchored top-third. Never a cropped desktop.

## Binding boundaries (from the Codex pressure-test)

- **Fully live** means a deterministic exclusive GPU creation choreography (rift-flight pattern, curl-noise-masked), not a physically balanced orbit. No authored media as motion carrier; optional baked environment assets only.
- **Typography:** particle mass announces; pre-mounted, pre-measured DOM resolves. No engine glyph rendering. Fonts load while First Contact is visible; nothing ever waits on font discovery under a cover.
- **After Arrival** the full solver retires: reduced dust budget (~2–4k), uniform-driven light breathing, camera parallax. The living environment must fit a real frame budget (engine ≤ ~10–11 ms desktop; mobile quality tiers with DPR 1.0–1.5 and honest fps targets).
- **One clock.** Engine, camera, and DOM condensation derive from one 0→1 creation progress. No second ceremony: the legacy `transformTimeline → buildTimeline` sequence is replaced by one creation contract (reducer names may remain).
- **Honest fallbacks.** Reduced motion: skip beats 2–4, brief dark hold, identical semantic end state. Device loss mid-creation: under cover, one controlled reinit or an honest static formed GRAVITY — never a resumed broken simulation. Composition frozen for the whole creation transaction; re-measure at Arrival; input released only after final pose + director init.

## Art direction calls

- **Light temperature:** cold deep blue-black void and world ground; the warm accretion-core light survives into GRAVITY as its interior light source. The tension cold/warm is the world's palette.
- **Ivory/Fraunces retirement:** complete inside GRAVITY. The world gets its own typographic mass; no ALABASTER tokens, plates, scanfront, or signature IDs.
- **LENOX staging:** instrument-calm visor (no smile, single slow pulse) through Decision–Accretion; warmth returns only at Arrival. Staging profile, not a vessel reopening.
- **Sound:** none in slice 1. Opt-in sound is a separate later decision.
- **Protected:** `PLAY WITH ME -> WAKE LENOX -> materialization` and the WAKE settle-target authorization remain untouched; the collapse begins only after present/recognition.

## Out of scope for slice 1

ATELIER, SIGNAL, world registry, business demos, voice, broad AI layer, the universal switch runtime (seam only), commits/pushes/deploys/dependency changes.
