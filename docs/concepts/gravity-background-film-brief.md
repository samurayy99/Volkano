# Creative Brief: The GRAVITY Background Film

**For:** all team voices (Codex, ChatGPT, Fable). Imagination fully open. 2026-07-16.

## Context

LENOX is a silver sphere robot with a black glass visor and cyan eyes — a living AI interface. After a visitor wakes him, he opens GRAVITY: his own world, a dark living environment that sits BEHIND and AROUND a vertically scrolling website (headlines, text columns, cases scroll over it as a narrative layer; LENOX floats in front of it).

Mechanic (decided, copied from the Wallow reference): the background is a prerendered film, exported as ~200–450 WebP frames, drawn to a fixed full-viewport canvas. **The visitor's scroll position selects the frame** — scrolling means moving through the film, forwards and backwards. Apple-style scroll scrubbing. The film will be AI-generated (Veo/Runway) from prompts, so anything imaginable is allowed — photorealism, impossible physics, any material.

## The task

Pitch ONE background film concept that makes people go "wow" the moment they scroll.

Hard requirements:
1. **Five parts** — the film is one continuous journey in 5 chapters (they map to the site's 5 scroll sections). Part 1 is the resting state after LENOX opens the world; part 5 is the final contact/CTA section.
2. **Scrub-safe** — one continuous shot, slow constant evolution, no cuts, no camera shake, must look good played forwards AND backwards at any speed.
3. **Content-safe** — text must stay readable on top: the composition needs calm zones; overall dark-leaning so white/light typography works.
4. **Desktop 16:9 and a separately composed 9:16 mobile version** (not a crop).
5. **LENOX must belong** — the world should feel like the home of this calm, precise, silver-and-cyan being, and it must feel ALIVE (idle motion even without scrolling).

Completely free: substance/material, palette, light, structure, choreography, mood, level of abstraction. It does NOT have to be cosmic or dust — surprise us. What matters: color, texture, choreography, and a dramaturgy across the 5 parts (beginning ≠ end; the journey transforms).

## Deliver

1. Concept name + one-sentence thesis
2. The 5 parts: what the viewer sees in each, and how each part FEELS
3. Palette (specific colors) + texture/structure language
4. Why it wows — what is the one image people screenshot?
5. Draft AI-video prompt (English) for part 1 and part 3 as proof the concept is generatable
6. One risk of the concept and how to soften it
