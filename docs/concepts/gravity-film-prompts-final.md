```

substance only: an immense obsidian-black ocean whose material sits between

liquid-silver capillaries live beneath the surface and remain attached to the

Sparse deep-cyan light (#35E7FF) exists only INSIDE the material as veins and
slow pressure pulses — never neon, never fog, never interface graphics.

Camera: locked, perfectly stabilized, fixed lens, fixed orientation. No cuts,
no shake, no zoom, no roll, no focus hunting, no lateral drift, no sudden
events. Motion is extremely slow, heavy, low-frequency and coherent: only a
few large connected surfaces stretch, fold, curve or smooth. Nothing splashes,
drips, tears, fragments or forms tendrils. Every movement must remain elegant
and physically believable played FORWARD, BACKWARD, or paused on any frame —
this footage is scroll-scrubbed in both directions.

Composition: dark-leaning, premium, editorial. Always keep one large calm
near-black negative-space zone for white typography. Do NOT render any robot,
character, figure, face, eyes, creature, text, logo, watermark or UI — a live
silver spherical AI character is composited in afterwards.

Palette only: abyss black #020406, obsidian #070A0E, black glass #0C1218,
graphite #172029, smoked steel #394650, liquid silver #C7D0D7, silver edge
#E5EBEF, lenox cyan #35E7FF–#55E7F2, deep cyan #0B5A65. Strictly no amber,
orange, gold or warm light.

Excluded language: decorative particle fields, magical sparks, smoke
ornaments, stars, clouds, fog, dust, rain, foam, bubbles, ordinary ocean
waves, waterfalls, stone, basalt, ceramic, fabric, plastic, plants,
architecture rooms, transparent chambers, chrome machinery, dashboards,
glowing interface grids, portals, wormholes, explosions, lightning,
biological tentacles, flicker, unstable topology, duplicated structures,
spontaneous object creation, disappearing material.

Photoreal impossible material physics, premium large-format cinematography,
deep controlled blacks, restrained silver highlights, monumental scale,
minimal visual noise, filmic grain.
```
