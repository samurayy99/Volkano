# Creative Brief v2: LENOX & WORLD — Total Reinvention

**Date:** 2026-07-16. Volkan's direction change: the dark obsidian world is rejected on taste ("emo depro style"), and the robot-head vessel is open to replacement (its visor geometry reads as built-in sadness). This is an explicit product decision by the owner.

## What stays (the concept is untouchable)

- `PLAY WITH ME -> WAKE` — a visitor wakes a living AI character.
- The character opens ITS world: a living background film behind and around a vertically scrolling website (scroll-scrubbed, Wallow mechanic).
- The character is rendered/composited LIVE in front of the film — it is the persistent intelligent presence across the whole site, never baked into the video.
- 5 scroll chapters mapping to the site sections, ending in contact/CTA.
- Desktop 16:9 and separately composed mobile 9:16.

## What is now COMPLETELY free

1. **The character** — any form. Does not need to be a robot, a head, or a sphere. Must be able to feel alive, express attention and joy, and work at small mobile scale in front of the film.
2. **The world** — any substance, palette, mood.

## The new taste law (from Volkan's heart)

- **Bright, colorful, alive, refreshing** — the energy of the Wallow reference (sunlit water, blooming pigment, joy), NOT dark/emo/depressive.
- Users must say: **"wow, I've never seen anything like this."**
- It must look galactic on BOTH mobile and desktop.

## Hard requirements (unchanged physics)

1. Five parts, one continuous journey, scrub-safe forwards AND backwards, no cuts, no camera shake.
2. Content-safe: calm zones for typography (note: bright worlds likely mean DARK type — plan the contrast).
3. Character never rendered inside the film.
4. Mobile 9:16 is a native recomposition, not a crop.
5. Idle life even without scrolling.

## Deliver per team member: TWO versions

Each version = **World + Character as one combination**, with:
1. Name + one-sentence thesis
2. The character: form, material, how it expresses aliveness/emotion without a sad face
3. The world film: palette (hex), substance, the 5-part arc in one line each
4. The wow moment — the screenshot people share
5. One risk + mitigation
A version may have a story or just be the hottest-looking thing imaginable. Boldness wins.
