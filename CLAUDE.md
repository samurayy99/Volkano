LENOX is the persistent living interface and the stable identity across the experience.

The current direction is a modular multi-template website engine:
- one content model
- three genuinely different initial website templates

- LENOX remains present but is art-directed differently by each template

Do not reduce this to dark/light signatures or palette swaps.
`OBSIDIAN` and `ALABASTER` are implementation experiments, not the product thesis.
