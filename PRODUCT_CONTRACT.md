# VOLKAN AI STUDIO — MASTER PRODUCT CONTRACT v1.2

**Status:** ACTIVE — approved by Volkan for implementation on 2026-07-24.
**Owner:** Volkan Kaymaz.

This contract is the canonical source for product positioning, the public
experience and launch scope. Existing architecture, security, engine, agent and
repository contracts remain binding unless this document explicitly changes
them. Historical product documents remain evidence, not active direction.

## Leadership decisions

1. Paying Swiss customers lead. Awards are a consequence of excellent work,
   never the product driver.
2. `SIGNAL` is the public default for every visitor without an explicit
   `?design=` selection.
3. The homepage has no gate or mandatory intro. The existing black
   `PLAY -> WAKE` experience is preserved for a later `/lab` route and curated
   entry, but is not mounted on `/`.
4. AI agents are functional systems with explicit scopes and limits, never
   fake human employees.
5. The public operating claim is `AI-OPERATED. HUMAN-DIRECTED.` No "first in
   Switzerland" or percentage claim ships without verified telemetry.
6. The three realities are `SIGNAL`, `GRAVITY` and `ATELIER`. `REEF` is a legacy
   identifier only and maps to `GRAVITY`.
7. LENOX is the studio operating intelligence. The authored v006 character is
   not persistent on the public homepage; it remains available for `/lab` and a
   later Volkan-approved signature reveal.

## Positioning

- Hero: `WEBSITES PEOPLE REMEMBER. AI SYSTEMS THAT KEEP WORKING.`
- Operating promise: `AI-OPERATED. HUMAN-DIRECTED.`
- Reality switch: `ONE INTELLIGENCE. THREE REALITIES.`
- Meta: `THE STUDIO IS THE DEMO.`

The sellable directions are Signature Website, Website Intelligence and Applied

## One truth, three realities

Factual claims, project status, offers, results, responsibilities and AI output
remain identical. Information order, emphasis, progressive disclosure,
navigation, typography, layout, motion, spatial composition and LENOX's
embodiment may change.

### SIGNAL — default

Swiss precision and commercial clarity. SIGNAL must explain the offer, human
responsibility and next action within the first viewport. LENOX appears as a
typographic analyst and guide; no character body or WebGPU dependency.

### GRAVITY — signature

Spatial, mass-driven, computational and real-time. No fantasy landscape,
coral-world, decorative monolith, generic neural-network graphic or background
film as core architecture. GRAVITY uses the existing WebGPU field without a
persistent character. Its material palette and light temperature remain pending
three Volkan-reviewed keyframes.

### ATELIER — artifact-led editorial

Real project artifacts, design decisions, typography and editorial composition
lead. LENOX curates and annotates; no character body or WebGPU dependency.

## Reality switching

The switch preserves semantic position, locale, selected case and current
interaction state without a reload. The visible transition is a two-phase Cut,
not a theme-picker wipe or simple crossfade. Its implementation technique is the
Tech Lead's decision. Reduced motion reaches the same final state.

`LET LENOX DECIDE` asks one question: `WHAT MUST YOUR WEBSITE PROVE FIRST?`

- `TRUST -> SIGNAL`
- `INNOVATION -> GRAVITY`
- `CHARACTER -> ATELIER`

LENOX returns one short reason. No behavioral profiling or cursor psychology.

## Truth and AI

All AI-facing states use honest labels such as `LIVE`, `SIMULATION`,
`HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`, `APPROVED` and `FAILED SAFELY`. No invented results,
awards, testimonials, client status or live activity.

Website Intelligence remains a disclosed frontend prototype until its separate
security and threat-model contract is approved. No live crawler, simulator,
calendar, outreach or agent backend belongs to Phase 1.

## Runtime laws

- Extend `src/app/studio`; no new app root or parallel state architecture.
- One `FlowEngineBridge`, one WebGPU renderer and one render loop.
- Existing reducer, provider, semantic position and design registry remain the
  authoritative infrastructure.
- No new state library, WebGL second path, dependency or package-manager change.
- `?design=signal|gravity|atelier` is canonical. `reef` and `?reality=` are
  accepted as legacy input only and are never emitted.
- Without WebGPU the complete semantic experience remains usable.
- Volkan alone owns visual acceptance. Agents provide technical evidence and a
  deterministic manual review harness; they do not perform browser automation
  or visual acceptance for this phase.

## Active scope — Phase 1 Reality Proof Slice

The existing full Studio journey remains. In development,
`?proof=realities` exposes one compact shared-content slice rendered as three
genuinely different compositions. It proves only:

1. the realities read as different websites;
2. input and an opened proof-analysis result survive switching;
3. the Cut is a signature transition with last-request-wins behavior;
4. mobile is independently composed.

The proof data is labelled `PROOF FIXTURE · NOT FOR PUBLIC RELEASE`. It is never
presented as client work. `/lab`, final art direction, full reality builds, live
AI and sales operations are explicitly deferred.
